UI/UX Case Study

Lexicon

An intelligent learning ecosystem that brings knowledge, skills and guidance together — helping users discover, learn and master new skills without leaving the app.

01 — The problem

Learning today is fragmented,
overwhelming and often ineffective

Most learners aren't short of material — they're short of structure. The friction sits between the tools, not inside them.

  • They rely on multiple platforms for courses, notes and doubt-solving
  • Switching between apps breaks focus and reduces learning continuity
  • There is no personalized learning path based on individual skill levels
  • Progress tracking is unclear, making it hard to stay motivated
  • Learners lack instant access to guidance when they get stuck
02 — The solution

One platform, one continuous path

Lexicon brings courses, progress tracking and teacher interaction into a single AI-powered platform. The solution focuses on clarity, continuity and personalization so learners stay engaged and actually finish what they start.

1

Unified learning platform

Course discovery, lessons, progress and doubt-solving all x-live in one app — no switching between platforms.

2

Personalized experience

AI-driven recommendations suggest courses and lessons based on skill level, interests and learning behaviour.

3

Simplified discovery

Clear categories and curated course lists cut decision fatigue at the moment of choosing what to learn next.

4

Visible progress

Progress indicators, milestones and completion status give learners a reason to come x-back tomorrow.

5

In-app teacher access

Direct access to teachers resolves doubts instantly, without the learner ever leaving the platform.

03 — Research

Understanding how people
actually learn online

The objective was to understand how learners currently discover, consume and manage online learning content, and to identify the challenges they face — lack of structure, low motivation, app switching and unclear progress.

General experience

  • Tell us about the last time you enrolled in an online course.
  • How do you usually choose what to learn next?
  • What platforms do you currently use for learning?

Pain points

  • Do you use more than one app while learning?
  • Have you ever stopped a course midway? Why?
  • What makes it difficult to stay consistent?

Progress & motivation

  • How do you track your learning progress today?
  • Do progress indicators motivate you to continue?
  • What makes you feel accomplished while learning?

Doubt solving

  • How do you clear doubts while learning?
  • Do you feel a lack of guidance or mentorship?
  • Would direct access to a teacher help you learn better?

Expectations

  • What would make a learning app feel easy and engaging?
  • What features would encourage you to complete courses?
  • What would make you trust and stick with one platform?
Learners are more likely to stay consistent when the experience is structured and guided rather than open-ended.
Visible progress tracking plays a key role in motivating users to continue and complete courses.
Reducing app switching significantly improves focus and learning continuity.
