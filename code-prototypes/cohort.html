Clinical signals
Cognitive/Memory fastest growing +118% since 2019
As the 50-59 cohort ages forward, demand for memory care, cognitive assessment, and Alzheimer's-related services will accelerate significantly over the next decade.
Vitamin D deficient in 64% of Vitamin D panels
621 patients have documented Vitamin D deficiency. Low Vitamin D is associated with higher MS relapse risk and worse cognitive outcomes — a modifiable, underaddressed risk factor.
JCV antibody positivity (38%) requires action
310 positive JCV results require immediate natalizumab risk stratification. JCV index >0.9 significantly increases PML risk.
Depression and anxiety affect 28% of the cohort
AAN quality measures require depression screening for MS, epilepsy, Parkinson's, stroke, and headache patients. 34,635 patients carry psychiatric comorbidity.
Stroke patients carry CCI 4.8 — highest in the cohort
Nearly double the cohort average. Multi-specialty coordination is essential — these patients are highest risk for adverse outcomes.
Operational signals
New consult cancel rate is 11.2% — highest of any type
New consultations have the highest cancellation rate and second-highest no-show rate. Dedicated reminder protocols and shorter wait-times could materially recover lost capacity.
73% lab ordering variation across providers
P86 orders 7.1 labs/patient while P274 orders 4.1 — a 73% difference. AAN Axon Registry data shows this is not correlated with practice size and warrants clinical audit.
Infusion visits grew 70% since 2019 — capacity pressure ahead
Natalizumab and ocrelizumab infusion growth is accelerating. Dedicated infusion chair and nursing capacity planning is now a near-term operational priority.
15% of cohort is lapsed >18 months — re-engagement opportunity
18,519 patients not seen in over 18 months. Proactive outreach — particularly for MS or progressive conditions — may prevent delayed diagnosis of relapses.
Ocrelizumab grew 415% since 2019 — fastest DMT adoption
Ocrevus adoption is outpacing all other DMTs. Infusion scheduling, ARIA monitoring, and formulary management planning should reflect this trajectory.
